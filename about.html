My Gaming development Goals

I am a student at University of Baguio whos passionate about building fun and intriguing games. Below are my three Gaming development goals.

  • Responsive Character Controller:instantly turns button presses into character movement without lag or glitching through walls.
  • Deterministic Game Logic:ensures the exact same player inputs will always create the exact same game results every single time.
  • Level of Detail (LOD):lowers a 3D object's graphics quality when far away to save computers processing power.
